{# How to fix #}

How to fix low DPI

Option 1: Resize the image

If you have the original high-res file, export or resize it to a larger pixel dimension. For a 6×9" cover at 300 DPI, you need 1800×2700 pixels minimum.

Option 2: Use a smaller print size

If your current pixels can't reach 300 DPI, consider a smaller trim size. A 5×8" cover needs only 1500×2400 pixels for 300 DPI.

{# Why DPI matters #}

Why 300 DPI matters

At 300 DPI

Your cover looks crisp and professional when printed. Text is sharp, images are clear, colors are vibrant. Readers see a quality product.

Below 300 DPI

Your cover looks pixelated or blurry. Text appears fuzzy. Images have visible blocks or artifacts. Readers think it's amateurish. KDP may reject it.
