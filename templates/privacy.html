Privacy

Your files, your data.

Plain-English summary of what we collect and what we don't. No lawyer-speak.

Files you upload

Manuscripts and covers you upload are analyzed in your browsing session and deleted the moment the check is complete. We do not store, read, share, or keep copies of your book files.

Email

We only collect your email address if you choose to enter it in a signup form. It's used to send occasional KDP tips and product updates, and it's handled by our email provider. You can unsubscribe at any time. We never sell your email.

Analytics

We use privacy-respecting website analytics to understand which tools are used and improve the site. This may set cookies and collects standard, non-identifying data such as pages viewed and general location. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Affiliate links

Some outbound links to third-party tools and services are affiliate links. If you buy or hire through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. This is disclosed where it applies and never affects the results a check gives you.

What we never do

  • Store your uploaded manuscripts or covers.
  • Sell or rent your personal data.
  • Ask for your KDP or Amazon login (we never need it).
