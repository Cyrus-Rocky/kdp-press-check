Required Elements

Copyright Page Essentials

Fiction Books

Must include:

  • Copyright © [Year] [Author Name]
  • ISBN (if applicable)
  • Disclaimer: This is a work of fiction. Any resemblance to real persons is coincidental.

Optional:

  • Publisher name
  • Edition info
  • Series name & book number

Non-Fiction Books

Must include:

  • Copyright © [Year] [Author Name]
  • ISBN (if applicable)
  • Disclaimer of liability (recommended)

Optional:

  • Publisher name
  • Edition info
  • Credential/expertise statement

Copyright vs. Trademark

Copyright © (what you use) protects the written work.

Trademark ™ (not needed here) protects brand names/logos.

Only use © on the copyright page. Your book title doesn't need ™.
