Review Timeline Guide

Calm your nerves during KDP review.

The KDP review process feels slow and mysterious. Here's exactly what's happening, how long it takes, what can go wrong, and what to do if it does.

The KDP Review Timeline

1

You Submit Your Book

Duration: Immediate

You hit "Save and Publish." Instantly, your book enters the KDP review queue. No delay here.

2

Automated Scanning (5-30 min)

Duration: 5-30 minutes

KDP's automated systems scan your file for obvious issues: corruption, missing fonts, blocked tags, copyright mismatches. If it fails here, you get an error immediately.

3

In Queue for Manual Review (1-24 hours)

Duration: Usually 12-48 hours (but can be up to 5 business days)

Your book joins the human review queue. During business hours (US time), this is often faster. Weekends and holidays slow things down.

4

Human Review (Varies)

Duration: Minutes to hours

A KDP reviewer checks your interior and/or cover against KDP's publishing guidelines. They verify margins, fonts, image quality, copyright page, metadata, etc.

5

Approval or Rejection

Duration: Immediate

KDP emails you with approval or rejection. Approval usually takes 24-72 hours total. Rejection includes a reason.

6

Post-Approval (48-72 hours)

Duration: 24-72 hours

Important: Approval ≠ visible on Amazon. Your book is approved but not yet searchable. This is normal and expected.

The book will show up in search results within 24-72 hours after approval. Author/publisher pages update separately.

What's Normal (Don't Panic)

✅ Status: "In review"

This is expected and takes up to 72 hours. Check back in 24 hrs.

✅ Book not showing in search yet

After approval, give it 24-72 hours. This is normal. Your book isn't invisible—it's indexing.

✅ Author page not updated yet

Author pages refresh separately from book availability. Can take extra 24 hours.

✅ Book in series not auto-linked

Series linking happens after books are live and indexed (~48-72 hrs).

✅ Slow review on weekends

KDP reviews slower on Saturdays, Sundays, and US holidays. Expected.

✅ No email = still reviewing

Silence is not rejection. You'll get an email (approval or rejection) within 72 hours.

If Your Book Gets Rejected

Step 1: Read the Error Message

KDP's error messages are vague, but they point to a real issue. Common reasons:

  • Margin error - Interior margins don't match KDP's rules for your page count
  • Image quality - DPI too low (must be 300 DPI minimum)
  • Page size mismatch - Your PDF doesn't match the trim size you selected
  • Copyright/metadata - Missing copyright info or metadata doesn't match file
  • Formatting issue - Corrupted fonts, unsupported characters, or broken links

Step 2: Use the Error Decoder

If KDP's message is cryptic, paste it into our Error Decoder for a plain-English translation and fix suggestions.

Step 3: Fix It

Go back to your interior/cover file, fix the issue, and reupload. Don't ask for a manual review yet—fix it first. Testing with our checklist tools helps:

  • Interior Check - Verify margins, fonts, page count
  • Cover Check - Verify dimensions, bleed, barcode

Step 4: Resubmit

Upload the fixed file and resubmit for review. Same 24-72 hour timeline applies. Most books are approved on the second attempt.

When to Ask for Manual Review

You can request a manual review if:

  • You've resubmitted 2+ times and still get rejected
  • The error message doesn't match what you see in your file
  • You believe KDP made a mistake

How: In your KDP Dashboard, find the book → "Paperback Details" or "Ebook Details" → Look for "Contact us about this issue" link.

Common Myths & Truths

🚫 Myth: "My book is banned"

Truth: Rejection ≠ ban. You can resubmit unlimited times. Only books with genuine KDP violations get removed after approval.

🚫 Myth: "I should wait longer"

Truth: No—if it's been 5+ business days and no email, contact KDP. It's a queue system, not a timer.

🚫 Myth: "Rejections hurt your account"

Truth: Rejections don't damage your account. Submit again. KDP reviewers don't hold grudges.

✅ Truth: "Resubmitting is fast"

Once you resubmit a fixed file, it goes back to the front of the queue. Usually approved within 24 hours.

✅ Truth: "Search indexing takes time"

After approval, your book isn't searchable for 12-72 hours. This is normal. Don't panic.

✅ Truth: "Pricing changes don't require reapproval"

Change your price, royalty option, or keywords anytime. No new review needed after approval.

Checklist: Before You Submit

Interior (if applicable)

  • ☐ Margins match KDP's requirements for your page count (Margin Advisor)
  • ☐ All images are 300 DPI minimum
  • ☐ Page count matches PDF (no blank pages)
  • ☐ Fonts are embedded (not system fonts)
  • ☐ Copyright page present with ISBN (if you have one)

Cover (if applicable)

  • ☐ Cover dimensions match trim size
  • ☐ Bleed/safe zones correct (Cover Check)
  • ☐ Barcode present (for print) or area reserved
  • ☐ Image quality is 300 DPI minimum

Metadata

  • ☐ Title matches cover and copyright page
  • ☐ Description is compelling and 100+ chars
  • ☐ Keywords filled (7 slots)
  • ☐ Category selected
  • ☐ Series info consistent across books (if applicable)
