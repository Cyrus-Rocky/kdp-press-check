{# Why It Matters #}

Why spine width matters

The real problem

You design your cover with a spine width that looks right on your screen. But if you change your paper type from white to cream (because cream looks better), the spine width mathematically changes. Your cover no longer fits KDP's exact dimensions. They reject it.

The fix

Calculate your spine width BEFORE you design the cover. Then, if you decide to change paper type, recalculate. Your designer can adjust the spine proportionally. This tool does the math so you don't lose weeks to rejection cycles.
