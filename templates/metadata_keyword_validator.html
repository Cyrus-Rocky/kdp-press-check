{# Why It Matters #}

Why KDP rejects keyword-stuffed subtitles

❌ Rejected Example

"Weight Loss Cookbook: Easy Healthy Recipes, Meal Plan, Low Carb, High Protein, Air Fryer, Diabetic Friendly, Quick Meals for Busy Families, 2026 Edition"

KDP sees this as: "You're trying to rank for every keyword imaginable instead of describing your actual book."

✅ Accepted Example

"Weight Loss Cookbook: Quick, Healthy Recipes for Busy Families"

KDP sees this as: "A clear description of what the book contains."

{# Tips #}

How to write a good subtitle

✍️ Describe, don't list

Your subtitle should tell readers what the book is about in plain English, not read like a search query.

🎯 Keep it short

60-80 characters is ideal. Longer than that, you're probably adding fluff or keywords.

💰 The payout

A good subtitle gets APPROVED on first upload. A bad one gets REJECTED and costs you a month.
