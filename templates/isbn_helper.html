Do You Need an ISBN?

📱 KDP Ebook Only

Need ISBN? No — KDP gives you a free one (ASIN)

Amazon's ASIN (Amazon Standard Identification Number) is your unique ID for ebooks. Free, automatic, works everywhere on Amazon.

📖 KDP Print (Paperback/Hardcover)

Need ISBN? Yes — you have 2 options:

  • Free ISBN from KDP: KDP Print will assign you a free ISBN. It only works on Amazon (not libraries, bookstores). ✅ Best if you're Amazon-exclusive.
  • Buy your own ISBN from Bowker: $119 for a single ISBN (or $295 for 10). Works on Amazon, IngramSpark, bookstores, libraries. ✅ Best if you want wide distribution.

⚠️ Important: You can only use one ISBN per book version. If you use KDP's free ISBN, you can't also sell the same version on IngramSpark.

🌍 Going Wide (Apple Books, B&N, Google Play, IngramSpark)

Need ISBN? Yes — buy your own

These platforms require an ISBN-13. You must buy it from Bowker (or a reseller like ISBN Search). Free KDP ISBN won't work.

Cost: ~$119/ISBN. Budget ~$300-500 if you're publishing 3+ books.

📚 Print-on-Demand Beyond KDP (IngramSpark, Lightning Source)

Need ISBN? Yes — bring your own

These platforms don't assign ISBNs; you must provide one. Buy from Bowker or reseller.

ISBN Myth-Busting

🚫 Myth: "I need an ISBN for Amazon ebook"

False. Amazon uses ASINs for ebooks, not ISBNs. KDP assigns a free ASIN automatically. ISBNs are for print books only.

🚫 Myth: "KDP's free ISBN works everywhere"

False. KDP's free ISBN only works on Amazon. Bookstores, libraries, and wide-distribution platforms reject it. You need Bowker's ISBN for that.

🚫 Myth: "ISBN costs $1,000"

False. Bowker (the official US ISBN authority) charges $119 for one ISBN. You don't need an ISBN to publish on Amazon KDP (free ebook or free print ISBN).

✅ Fact: "One ISBN = one version"

Each format (paperback 5x8", hardcover 6x9", ebook, audiobook) needs its own ISBN. You can't reuse the same ISBN for different versions.

Quick Decision Tree

Are you publishing ONLY on Amazon Kindle (ebook)?

→ No ISBN needed. KDP gives you a free ASIN. Move on.

Are you publishing a paperback/hardcover on KDP Print?

→ Choose one: Use KDP's free ISBN (Amazon-only) OR buy your own (for wider distribution). Can't do both.

Do you want to sell on bookstores, libraries, or IngramSpark?

→ Buy your own ISBN from Bowker ($119). KDP's free ISBN won't work.

Are you publishing multiple formats (ebook + paperback + hardcover)?

→ Budget: Each format needs its own ISBN (if print). Ebook doesn't need one (use ASIN).
