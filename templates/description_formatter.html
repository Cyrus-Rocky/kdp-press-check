<u>...</u> — Underline (rare).
<u>Underlined</u>

❌

❌
Event handlers: onclick, onload, onerror, etc. Security risk, always blocked.

Tips for Great Descriptions

1. Hook readers immediately

Start with a question, surprising fact, or emotional hook. You have 100 characters before the "Read more" cutoff.

2. Use short paragraphs

One idea per paragraph. Wrap in <p> tags to add space. Blocks of text scare readers on mobile.

3. Bold key selling points

Use <strong> sparingly—2-3 times max. Highlight: "Award-winning author" or "Perfect for fans of X."

4. Avoid hype words

Skip: "Bestseller," "Award-winning" (unless true), "Must-read." Let the story speak.

5. End with a CTA or hook

Final sentence should make readers want to buy: "Find out..." or "Discover..." or "Scroll up to grab your copy."

6. Test on mobile

Most readers browse descriptions on phones. Short paragraphs + line breaks make it readable.
